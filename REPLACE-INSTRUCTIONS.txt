Replace these files in the same folders:

handlers/submit-lead.php
includes/integrations/SmtpMailer.php
includes/integrations/ClickUpClient.php
assets/js/main.js
integration-test.php

Do not replace config.local.php. Your existing SMTP and ClickUp keys remain there.

After upload:
1. Clear Hostinger cache and browser cache.
2. Open /integration-test.php once.
3. It prints the exact SMTP and ClickUp error or SUCCESS.
4. Delete integration-test.php after testing.
5. Check storage/logs/forms-YYYY-MM.log after a form submission.

Required permissions:
storage 775
storage/logs 775
storage/rate-limits 775
uploads 775
